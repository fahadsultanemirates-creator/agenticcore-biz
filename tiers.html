Choosing the right tier

Low, mid, or high — what actually changes

Every plan comes in three tiers. Here's what genuinely differs between them, so you're never guessing.

Low Tier

Essential & hands-off

For businesses testing the waters — basic social handling with limited tools, no frills.

  • Basic social media handling on your core platform(s)
  • Limited toolset, standard agent workflow
  • Handled by our standard agent workflow
  • 2 free revision rounds
Mid Tier

Balanced & planned

The right fit for most real businesses — a real plan behind the work, plus your first free AI agent.

  • A feasibility & planning pass before anything launches
  • 1 free AI agent, scoped to a task you choose
  • Auto-posting across your active platforms
  • Select AgenticCore Agency tasks included free — a simple logo or image, for example
  • Discounted AgenticCore Agency website help
  • 2 free revision rounds
High Tier

Full framework & monitored

For businesses ready to run marketing on autopilot, watched in real time.

  • Full auto-posting across every platform
  • A complete AI marketing framework, built and maintained by us
  • Real-time performance monitoring and adjustment
  • 50% off any AgenticCore Agency project
  • 2 free revision rounds

Who picks the tier?

You do — always. When you submit a New Request, the AI agent scoping your project will suggest a tier based on your product, website, and competition, but the final choice is yours. There's no penalty for choosing lower than recommended; you'll simply get a faster, more standardized version of what you asked for.

Why high tier costs more

Higher tiers aren't just "the same work with a higher price tag." They come with a full AI framework built and maintained for you, real-time monitoring, and the deepest discount on AgenticCore Agency work. As AgenticCore grows, new and more advanced agents will be introduced specifically at the high tier first.

Revisions work the same across every tier — two free rounds included, regardless of what you choose.

Paid Media & Optimization

Ad spend that gets watched and adjusted continuously, not set once at the start of the month and checked at the end of it.

What it is

Predictive Audience Targeting & Setup

Ad audiences built from data and modeling rather than platform defaults or guesswork about who your customer "probably" is.

How it works

Using your existing customer data (or close proxies if you're just starting), we model the traits that actually predict a good customer and configure your ad platforms' targeting around that — a one-time setup that changes what your budget is actually being shown to.

Why it matters

Most ad budget waste happens before an ad is even seen by the wrong person — it's spent showing it to them at all. Getting targeting right at setup is the highest-leverage fix available, before a single dollar of ongoing spend.

What it is

Autonomous Ad Budget Allocation

A system that shifts your ad budget toward whatever's actually performing, continuously — across campaigns, platforms, or audiences — instead of a fixed monthly split decided in advance.

How it works

Running monthly against your live campaigns, the system tracks performance in real time and reallocates spend toward what's converting, pulling back from what isn't, faster than a manual weekly review ever could.

Why it matters

A budget split decided at the start of the month is already stale by week two. Continuous reallocation means underperforming spend gets caught and redirected in days, not discovered at the next reporting cycle.

What it is

Algorithmic A/B Testing & CRO

Ongoing, systematic testing of your landing pages and ad variants — headlines, layouts, offers — to find what actually converts, run as a continuous process rather than a one-off test.

How it works

Running monthly, variants are tested against live traffic with statistically sound splits, results are analyzed automatically, and winning versions are rolled out — with the next round of tests already queued up.

Why it matters

Conversion rate optimization done as an occasional project stalls the moment the project ends. Treating it as a continuous, monthly process is what actually compounds a percentage point of conversion into real revenue over time.

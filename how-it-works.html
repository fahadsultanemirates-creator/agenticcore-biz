The dashboard, explained

How AgenticCore Biz actually works

One dashboard. Five sections. No guessing what happens after you sign up.

01

My Projects — your home screen

This is what you see the moment you log in. Every plan you have — active, awaiting your review, or already running — is listed here with a clear status, so you never have to message us just to ask "where's my campaign at?"

  • See status at a glance: In Progress, Awaiting your review, Revision requested, or Delivered
  • Tap into any project to see full detail, files, and the conversation history behind it
  • Nothing here needs a support ticket — it's designed to answer the question before you have to ask it
02

New Request — where a plan actually starts

Describe your business in plain language — no forms, no jargon. Before we recommend anything, an AI agent assesses your product, your website, and your competition, then presents a clear, written plan for you to confirm before anything is charged.

  • The agent scopes a single, clear plan based on the assessment — no tiers to choose between
  • Nothing starts until you've approved the plan in writing — this protects you from surprises and us from misunderstandings
  • Once confirmed and paid, your project gets a name automatically and appears in My Projects
  • Every finalized request also reaches a real person directly — while our automated project manager is still being perfected, a human confirms and oversees every task personally
03

Services — everything we offer, without the clutter

Rather than a page crammed with every service and price, Services is a simple overview. See what's included by category, and jump straight into a request when you're ready.

  • Social media, AI marketing agents, feasibility & planning, and more — organized by category, not buried in a wall of text
  • Need site work too? Discounted and free website help is handled by our sister company, AgenticCore Agency — linked directly from here
04

Billing — everything in one place

Pay directly from the dashboard — no separate invoicing portal to dig through. À la carte services follow a simple split: 30% upfront gets work started, the remaining 70% is due once it's delivered and you've reviewed it, so you're never paying the full amount blindly before seeing real progress. The two standard packages bill monthly instead, at the start of each cycle.

05

Business Pool — for teams who work with us often

Once your spend with us crosses a set threshold, you're automatically eligible for a separate, elevated experience — faster delivery, a dedicated human manager, and real discounts. Read the full breakdown →

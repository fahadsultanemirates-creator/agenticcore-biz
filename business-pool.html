The Business Pool

A separate, elevated tier of service for companies who work with us regularly — not a loyalty badge, an actual operational upgrade.

How you qualify

Qualification is based on your total spend with us, not how many individual tasks you've submitted — so it reflects real relationship value, not just order volume.

  • Reach a cumulative spend threshold within a rolling period, or
  • Sign up directly for a monthly retainer covering ongoing work (social, AI agents, planning)
Once you qualify, you get access to a separate, dedicated view built specifically for Business Pool clients — distinct from the standard dashboard.

What actually changes

Once your spend with AgenticCore crosses $5,000, your account automatically upgrades to Business Pool — no application, no waiting on approval. It just happens.

What changes:

  • A dedicated human manager — Business Pool accounts get a direct Telegram line to a real person assigned to your account, not the AI agent queue everyone else uses
  • Faster delivery on every request
  • 20% off all services
  • A custom content package included monthly

Standard accounts stay connected through our AI agent specialist on Telegram — fast, always available, and perfect for day-to-day requests. Business Pool swaps that for a dedicated human manager who already knows your account and history.

Why this exists

Most of our clients need one plan executed well. Some need us running continuously alongside their business — marketing that never stops, campaigns that adjust in real time, a framework that stays current without anyone chasing it down. The Business Pool is built for that second kind of relationship, with the responsiveness and pricing to match.

Reach out and we'll check your eligibility — or start a monthly retainer directly.

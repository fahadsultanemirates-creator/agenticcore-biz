Lead Generation & Outreach

Finding the right people to talk to, and reaching them like a person would — at a volume no single salesperson could sustain.

What it is

Multi-Agent Lead Scraping & Enrichment

A batch of B2B leads matched to your Ideal Customer Profile (ICP) — not a generic list, and not just names and emails. Each lead is enriched with the context a real outreach attempt needs.

How it works

You define who you're actually trying to reach (industry, company size, role, region — whatever qualifies a real prospect for you). AI agents search, cross-reference, and verify 1,000 leads against that profile, then enrich each one with firmographic and contact detail.

Why it matters

A list of names isn't a pipeline. Verified, enriched leads matched to your actual ICP are what makes the next step — outreach that doesn't get ignored — possible at all.

What it is

Hyper-Personalized Cold Outreach

Full outbound infrastructure — not a mail-merge blast. Every message is written around that specific prospect, sent through infrastructure built to actually land in an inbox.

How it works

Running monthly against your lead list (ours or your own), each message references something real and specific about the recipient or their company, sequenced across multiple touches, with deliverability infrastructure (domain warm-up, sending rotation) managed for you.

Why it matters

Generic cold email gets ignored or flagged as spam before it's ever read. Personalization at this level, run consistently, is what actually gets replies — but it's not something one person can do by hand at real volume.

What it is

Automated Lead Qualification & Scoring

A system that sorts incoming leads by how likely they are to actually convert, so your (or your team's) time goes to the right conversations first.

How it works

Set up once against your definition of a "good" lead — firmographic fit, engagement signals, stated intent — the system scores every new lead as it comes in and flags the ones worth prioritizing.

Why it matters

Not every lead deserves the same amount of attention. Without scoring, real prospects sit in the same queue as tire-kickers, and follow-up happens on a first-come basis instead of a best-fit one.

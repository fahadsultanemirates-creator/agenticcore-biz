How working with AgenticCore Biz actually works — scoping, payment, revisions, and where responsibility sits on each side.

Last updated 15 September 2026

These terms describe how AgenticCore Biz actually operates today — the discovery-first process, the 30/70 payment split, the two free revisions, and USDT settlement. They are written to be read, not to be impenetrable. They have not yet been reviewed by a lawyer in your jurisdiction; have that done before you rely on them commercially.

1. Who we are

AgenticCore Biz ("AgenticCore Biz", "we", "us") provides AI-assisted marketing services — social media management, AI marketing agents, lead generation, paid media, organic growth, and planning and feasibility work. We are part of the AgenticCore group and operate alongside our sister company, AgenticCore Agency, which handles website builds, branding, and permanent AI-agent frameworks.

By creating an account, submitting a request, or paying an invoice, you agree to these terms. If you are agreeing on behalf of a company, you confirm you are authorised to bind it.

2. Accounts

You need an account to submit requests and track work. You are responsible for the accuracy of what you tell us and for keeping your login credentials secure. Tell us promptly if you believe someone else has accessed your account.

One person or company, one account. We may suspend or close accounts used to abuse the referral programme, to submit fraudulent payment claims, or to misuse the service.

3. How an engagement starts — nothing is priced blind

We do not sell work sight-unseen. Every engagement starts with a discovery conversation about your product, your website, and your competitors. You can start that through the New Request form, through Forge (our in-dashboard assistant), or through the chat on this site.

  • Prices published on this site are starting ranges for reference, not fixed quotes and not offers capable of acceptance.
  • After discovery we set an agreed price against your request. Your dashboard then shows that request as awaiting payment.
  • No work begins, and no money is due, until you have seen that agreed price and chosen to pay against it.
  • We may decline any request. Discovery conversations are free and create no obligation on either side.

4. Payment — 30% upfront, 70% on delivery

À la carte services are billed in two parts. 30% of the agreed price is payable upfront and is what starts the work. The remaining 70% falls due once the work has been delivered and you have approved it in your dashboard.

Monthly packages (AI Starter Engine and Omni-Scale Growth Engine) bill monthly in advance at the start of each cycle instead, and continue until cancelled. The Custom Package has no set price and is scoped and billed individually.

  • Payment is by USDT on the BEP20 (BNB Smart Chain) network. The receiving address and a QR code appear on the request itself once a price is agreed.
  • Cryptocurrency transfers are irreversible. Sending USDT on any other network, or sending a different token, will result in permanent loss of those funds, and we cannot recover or reimburse them.
  • Always verify the receiving address against the one shown in your dashboard at the moment you send. Never rely on an address given to you any other way.
  • After sending, email us your transaction hash so we can verify it on-chain. Work starts once we have confirmed receipt, not at the moment you send.
  • All prices are in US dollars. You bear network fees and any exchange-rate difference between the quoted USD figure and the USDT you send.
  • Taxes, duties, and withholdings applicable in your jurisdiction are yours to handle.
We never ask for payment by direct message, and we never change the receiving address mid-engagement without it also changing in your dashboard. If anyone contacts you claiming otherwise, it is not us — stop and email hello@agenticcore.biz before sending anything.

5. Delivery, revisions, and approval

When work is ready we mark the project delivered in your dashboard. From there you can either request a revision or approve it.

  • Every project includes two free revisions. A revision is a refinement within the agreed scope — not a new brief, a change of direction, or a fresh deliverable.
  • Further revisions, or changes that fall outside the agreed scope, are quoted and billed separately.
  • Approving a delivery raises the remaining 70% and moves the project to approved.
  • If you neither approve nor request a revision within 14 days of delivery, we may treat the work as accepted and raise the remaining balance.

6. What we need from you

Marketing work depends on inputs only you can provide. Delays in providing them are not delays we can absorb.

  • Timely access to accounts, assets, brand materials, and approvals where the work requires them.
  • Accurate information about your product, your claims, and your market. You are responsible for the truth of anything you ask us to publish about your business.
  • Confirmation that you hold the rights to any material you give us to use.
  • Your own compliance with the rules of any platform we publish to on your behalf, and with advertising, consumer-protection, and industry-specific law that applies to your business.

7. Results — what we do and do not promise

We commit to the planning, the execution, and the monitoring described in your agreed scope, carried out with reasonable skill and care. We do not guarantee any particular commercial outcome: not a volume of leads, a conversion rate, a ranking, a follower count, a reach figure, or revenue.

Marketing performance depends on your product, your pricing, your market, and platform algorithms and policies outside anyone’s control. Any figure, forecast, or example discussed during scoping is an illustration, not a commitment.

8. AI-generated work

Much of what we produce is generated or assisted by AI systems, including AI avatar video, ad creative, written content, and automated outreach. You should assume AI involvement in any deliverable unless we say otherwise.

AI output can be wrong, and it can resemble other material. We review what we publish, but you remain responsible for approving deliverables before they represent your business publicly. Tell us if your industry restricts AI-generated marketing, or if you require human-only production for particular assets.

9. The AI assistants on this site

Forge and the site chat assistant are automated. They help scope work and answer questions; they do not agree prices, accept contracts, or bind us to anything. Only a written agreed price in your dashboard does that.

Conversations with them are stored so the assistant remembers context between visits, and are readable by our team. Do not paste passwords, card numbers, private keys, or seed phrases into them — or anywhere else on this site.

10. Intellectual property

You keep everything you owned already: your brand, your content, your data, your accounts.

Deliverables we produce for you transfer to you once the work is paid for in full. Until then we retain them. What stays ours throughout are our own tools, prompts, workflows, agent frameworks, and internal methods — none of which are part of any deliverable.

Unless you ask us not to, we may reference that we worked with you, and show non-confidential examples of the work, in our own portfolio and marketing.

11. Confidentiality

Each side will keep the other’s non-public information confidential and use it only for the engagement. This does not cover information that is already public, that was already known, or that must be disclosed by law.

12. Cancellation

You can stop working with us at any time. The 30% upfront covers work already started and is non-refundable once work has begun. If you cancel mid-project, you owe the value of work completed to that point; anything paid beyond that is returned.

Monthly packages can be cancelled before the next cycle starts. The current cycle is not pro-rated. We may pause or stop work on overdue balances, or end an engagement where the request is unlawful, abusive, or outside what we can deliver responsibly.

Subject to that: we are not liable for lost profits, lost revenue, lost opportunity, or indirect or consequential loss; and our total liability for any engagement is capped at the amount you paid us for that engagement in the 6 months before the claim.

We are not liable for losses caused by cryptocurrency sent to a wrong address or on a wrong network, by third-party platforms suspending or restricting your accounts, or by changes to platform algorithms or policies.

Continuing to use the service after a change means you accept it. Work already scoped and paid for stays governed by the terms in force when it was agreed.

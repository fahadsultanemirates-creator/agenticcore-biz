Referral Points Policy

The complete rules for AgenticCore Points: how they are earned, what they are worth, and what will get them taken away.

Last updated 15 September 2026

This policy governs AgenticCore Points and the referral programme. It sits alongside our Terms of Service. It has not yet been reviewed by a lawyer in your jurisdiction — if you promote the programme publicly, have it checked against local rules on referral and promotional schemes first.

1. What Points are — and what they are not

One AgenticCore Point is worth $1 of credit against any AgenticCore Biz service.

  • Points are not cash, not a currency, not a token, and not a security or investment of any kind.
  • They cannot be withdrawn, exchanged for money, sold, or transferred to another account.
  • They carry no interest, no yield, and no value outside AgenticCore Biz.
  • They do not expire.
  • Points have no cash value on account closure and are forfeited when an account closes.
This is a customer rewards programme, not an earning opportunity. You earn Points only when real, paid work is completed — never for signing up, never for sharing a link, and never for referring someone who has not ordered anything.

2. How you earn Points

Points are credited automatically when a referred client’s project payment is recorded as paid. They are calculated on the value of that payment.

  • Level 1 — someone you referred directly: you earn 20% of their task value in Points.
  • Level 2 — someone they referred: you earn 10% of that task value in Points.
  • Level 3 — someone that person referred: you earn 5% of that task value in Points.
  • Being referred pays too — if you signed up through someone’s link, you earn 10% of your own task value back in Points.

3. The three-task limit

Every reward above applies only to each client’s first 3 completed, paid tasks. From their fourth task onward, that client’s work no longer generates Points for anyone in the chain — including themselves.

The chain is fixed at signup: it is determined by the referral link used to create the account and cannot be changed, backdated, or reassigned afterwards.

4. A worked example

You refer Sara. Sara refers Ali. Ali refers Nadia. Nadia orders a $500 plan, and it is completed and paid.

  • Nadia earns $50 in Points (10% of $500, because she was referred).
  • Ali earns $100 in Points (20% — Level 1 from Nadia).
  • Sara earns $50 in Points (10% — Level 2 from Nadia).
  • You earn $25 in Points (5% — Level 3 from Nadia).

5. Spending Points

Points are applied against the price of future AgenticCore Biz services. Tell us before payment is due that you want to use them, and we apply the balance to that invoice.

  • Points apply to service fees only — not to third-party costs we pass through, such as ad spend or platform fees.
  • Points can be combined with Business Pool pricing and with package pricing.
  • A Points balance larger than an invoice leaves the remainder in your account for next time. Points are never refunded as money.

6. Reversals

If a payment that generated Points is later refunded, reversed, charged back, or found to be fraudulent, the Points it generated are deducted from every account that received them. If a balance has already been spent, we may invoice for the difference.

7. Fair use

The programme rewards genuine introductions. We will remove Points, close accounts, and cancel balances where we find:

  • self-referral — referring yourself through a second account, a family member’s account, or a company you control, to claim both sides;
  • fake, duplicate, or automated account creation;
  • orders placed to generate Points with no genuine intent to have the work done;
  • spam, misleading claims, or impersonating AgenticCore to promote a link;
  • any other attempt to extract Points without a real, paid engagement behind them.

8. Changes to the programme

We may change the rates, the three-task limit, or the structure of the programme, or end it entirely. Changes apply going forward only: Points already earned remain valid and spendable under this policy, and work already in progress is credited at the rates in force when it started. Material changes are notified by email or in the dashboard.

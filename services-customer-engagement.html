Services / Customer Engagement

Customer Engagement

Keeping the customers you already have engaged, answered, and coming back — without needing someone watching every channel around the clock.

What it is

24/7 AI Sales & Support Chatbot

A chatbot on your site (and optionally WhatsApp/Telegram) that actually answers — product questions, pricing, order status, basic troubleshooting — any time, not just during business hours.

How it works

Built and trained on your actual business (products, policies, FAQs, tone), then set up once. From there it's a flat monthly cost to keep it running, monitored, and updated as your business changes.

Why it matters

Most inbound questions happen outside business hours or while your team is busy. A visitor who doesn't get an answer in the first few minutes usually doesn't come back — this closes that gap without a night shift.

What it is

Automated Review & Reputation Management

A system that requests reviews from happy customers at the right moment, monitors what's being said about you across platforms, and flags anything that needs a real response.

How it works

Review requests go out automatically after a completed purchase or service, timed to catch customers while the experience is fresh. New reviews and mentions are tracked monthly, with negative ones surfaced to you quickly rather than discovered by chance.

Why it matters

Most satisfied customers never leave a review unless asked at the right moment — and most businesses find out about a bad one too late to respond well. This fixes both sides of that.

What it is

Behavioral Re-engagement Workflows

Automated sequences that bring back customers who've gone quiet — an abandoned cart, a lapsed subscriber, a lead who stopped responding — triggered by their actual behavior, not a fixed calendar.

How it works

Set up once against the behaviors that matter for your business (cart abandonment, inactivity, a stalled deal), the workflow watches for those triggers and sends the right follow-up automatically, with no ongoing manual work after setup.

Why it matters

It's almost always cheaper to win back someone who already showed interest than to acquire a new customer from scratch — but that only happens if someone follows up, consistently, at the right moment. This makes sure that happens every time.

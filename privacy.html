Privacy Policy

What we collect, why we collect it, and how it's kept safe.

What we collect

  • Account information — your email and password (handled by our authentication provider; we never see or store your raw password).
  • Business & project details — the descriptions, goals, and materials you submit for any request, plus anything needed to run social/marketing work on your behalf (e.g. brand assets, competitor references).
  • Payment details — for USDT payments, the transaction hash and wallet details you share with us for manual verification. We don't process or store card numbers.
  • Communications — messages you send us through the dashboard, Telegram, WhatsApp, or the on-site chat widget, so we can respond and keep a record of what was agreed.
  • Referral activity — who referred you and who you've referred, so AgenticCore Points can be credited correctly.
  • Basic usage data — standard technical information (like browser type and general access logs) collected automatically to keep the service running and secure.

How we use it

Strictly to run your engagement: scoping and delivering the work you request, processing and verifying payment, tracking referrals and Points, and communicating with you about your account. We don't use your information for anything beyond operating AgenticCore Biz.

What we don't do

We don't sell your data. We don't share it with third parties except the service providers that keep AgenticCore Biz running (such as our database and authentication provider), and only to the extent needed to provide the service.

How long we keep it

We keep account and project data for as long as your account is active, and as needed to meet legal, accounting, or dispute-resolution obligations afterward. You can request deletion of your account and associated data at any time — see below.

Your choices

You can request a copy of the information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or request that your account and data be deleted, by contacting us at hello@agenticcore.biz.

Questions

If anything here is unclear, reach out any time at hello@agenticcore.biz or on Telegram.
